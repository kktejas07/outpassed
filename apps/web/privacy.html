Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Outpass is used by colleges, hostels and schools to approve student outpasses, record gate movements and manage visitors. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it. The institution that runs Outpass for you decides who has an account and is responsible for how its staff use the data.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number and role (student, parent, warden, guard or admin).
  • Student details: roll number, hostel, room and date of birth. Date of birth is used only to decide whether a parent's approval is required.
  • Outpass requests: destination, reason, leave and return times, and each approval or rejection with who made it.
  • Gate movements: exit and entry times, the gate, the guard who recorded it, and whether the guard confirmed the person's identity.
  • Visitors: the visitor's name, phone number, purpose, the person they're visiting and their arrival and departure times.
  • Notifications: a push token for your device, so we can send alerts, and the alerts themselves.

The camera is used only to scan QR codes at the gate. Outpass doesn't record or store camera images.

Why we use it

To run the approval chain, let guards verify passes, tell parents when their child leaves or returns, alert wardens about overdue students, let hosts approve visitors, and send password reset codes. We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Students see their own passes and visitors.
  • Parents see the passes of the students linked to them.
  • Wardens and admins at your institution see passes, gate movements and visitors for the campus.
  • Guards see the pass details needed to let someone in or out.
  • A visitor's pass link shows the visitor's name, the host's name, the time window and the entry code, but not phone numbers.

We use service providers to host the app, deliver push notifications (Expo, Apple and Google) and send email. They process data only to provide those services.

How long we keep it

Records are kept while your institution uses Outpass, so it can keep a movement register. Your institution can ask for records older than its retention period to be deleted.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings. To see, correct or delete your data, contact your institution's Outpass admin or write to us at the address below.

Children

Students under 18 use Outpass only through an institution, with a parent or guardian linked to their account.
