How an outpass works from request to return

Each person is asked only when it's their turn, and the student sees exactly where the request is.

  1. Student asks

    Picks why they're going out (sick, family trip, academic, personal) and who gave permission.

  2. Faculty approves

    The faculty member who gave permission says yes first. The department head is told.

  3. Parent approves

    Parents get a WhatsApp message and approve with one tap.

  4. Warden gives the final OK

    The pass turns live on the student's phone for the approved times.

  5. Guard scans out and in

    Exit and return are recorded with a photo. Everyone involved is told.

Emergency? It skips straight to the warden, and the parent, faculty member and department head are alerted at once.

Questions colleges ask

Do parents need to install the app?

No. Parents can approve requests from the WhatsApp message they receive. The app adds history, fees and alerts for those who want it.

What if the gate has no internet?

The guard's phone keeps checking gate passes and recording exits and returns offline. Everything is sent once the connection comes back.

Can students over 18 skip the parent step?

Yes, if your college chooses. You can keep parent approval for everyone, or only tell parents when an adult student leaves and returns.

Does it work for schools?

Yes. School mode hides offers and the marketplace, and asks a parent's permission before keeping any student's photo.

How do messages reach people?

Sign-in codes and alerts go over WhatsApp, with app notifications on top for people who have the app installed.

Can students pay fees in Outpass?

Yes. The office assigns fees, students or parents pay online or upload a payment screenshot, and reminders go out before and after the due date.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, Android and any web browser. The guard desk runs on any phone with a camera.
